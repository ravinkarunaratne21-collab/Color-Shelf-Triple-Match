How to play

Sort: Tap or click a movable item, then an empty slot. A shelf section holds 3 or 4 items.

Match: Put exactly three matching items in one section to clear them. Matches free shelf space.

Hidden: An item behind another is revealed when the front item is cleared.
Locked: Bring its two matching partners to the same section to clear the trio.
Frozen: Two matches in its section thaw it so it can be moved.

Space: Non-matching moves add shelf pressure. A match reduces it. Fill the meter and the shelf jams.

Themes change every 10 levels. The 100-level campaign gradually adds shelves, item types, obstacles, and tighter timers.
